Common questions

Is Skein free?

Yes — the core practice is free forever. The daily card, journal entries (with voice and photo capture), Pattern Mirror basics, the default journal theme, and one bundled deck are all included at no cost. Skein Plus is an optional subscription that unlocks journal search, archive export, Pattern Mirror Pro, unlimited physical decks, and the Stellar Atlas journal theme — see the home page for current pricing.

Does Skein work offline?

Yes. Skein is offline-first. Your daily card, journal entries, and pattern dashboard work entirely on your device. An account is only needed if you want to back up and sync across devices.

Does Skein interpret the cards for me?

No. Skein never tells you what a card means. We provide traditional symbolism, keywords, and reflection prompts — but the interpretation is always yours, written in your own words in the journal.

How does voice reflection work?

Tap the microphone button inside any reflection field. Your speech is transcribed by your device's built-in speech recognition (on-device on iOS 17+ where supported). Skein only ever sees the resulting text — the audio recording itself never leaves your phone. You can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in your device Settings; typing remains fully functional either way.

How do I cancel my Skein Plus subscription?

Apple and Google own the cancellation flow for subscriptions purchased through their stores. On iOS, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Skein and tap Cancel Subscription. On Android, open Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → Skein. You'll keep Plus access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Skein Plus screen (Settings → Cloud Sync, or the Membership option in your Profile) and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID / Google account you used for the original purchase — this also restores your Plus subscription on a new device.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Skein → your Profile → Delete Account (below Sign Out). This permanently removes your account and all its data from our servers, and clears the local copy from this device. It can't be undone.

I found a bug / I have a feature request

We love hearing both. Email us with "Bug" or "Idea" in the subject and as much detail as you can share.
